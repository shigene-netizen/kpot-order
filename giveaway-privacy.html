LUCKY 66 Giveaway Privacy Notice

This notice covers only the LUCKY 66 giveaway. It does not replace the privacy practices that apply when you order food from KPOT EXPRESS.

What we collect when you enter

  • Your e-mail address — used to send the verification link, your entry confirmation, and prize notification if you win.
  • Entry records: an entry reference, a one-way hash of your e-mail address (so duplicates can be detected without exposing the address), the time you entered, and which version of the rules you acknowledged.
  • Your optional choice about receiving marketing e-mail (off by default), with the time and version of the notice you were shown.

What we do not do

  • We never publish entrant e-mail addresses — not on the entry page, not in reports, not in exports.
  • We do not subscribe anyone to marketing e-mail for entering. Marketing consent is a separate, optional checkbox.
  • We do not use entrant data for ordering, loyalty points or any other shop account; giveaway records are kept separate from order and rewards records.

How long we keep it

Entry records are kept for the duration of the round and a reasonable period afterwards for prize fulfillment, audit and legal record-keeping, then deleted. [CONFIRM RETENTION PERIOD IN LEGAL REVIEW]

Your choices

You can withdraw marketing consent at any time through the unsubscribe link in any marketing e-mail (honored promptly) or by asking at the shop. Withdrawing consent never affects a giveaway entry.
