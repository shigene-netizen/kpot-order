KPOT EXPRESS — LUCKY 66 Giveaway

Sponsor: KHP ATLANTA LLC, operating as KPOT EXPRESS, 2955 COBB PKWY SE, 830, ATLANTA, GA 30339.

1. No purchase necessary

Participation is free. No purchase, payment, order, tip, donation or subscription of any kind is required or requested, and none will improve your chances of winning. A purchase does not increase your odds; entry is only by the free method described here. The giveaway is void where prohibited.

2. Eligibility

  • Open only to legal residents of the State of Georgia, United States who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry.
  • Employees of KPOT EXPRESS and its advertising/promotion agencies, and members of their immediate families, are not eligible.
  • Limit: one entry per person per round, identified by a unique verified e-mail address. Duplicate addresses do not receive additional entries.
  • You must confirm ownership of your e-mail address through the verification link sent to you. Unverified addresses never count toward the entry limit and are not eligible to win.

3. How to enter

  1. Open the LUCKY 66 page (https://order.kpotexpress.com/giveaway.html).
  2. Enter a valid e-mail address, confirm the eligibility statement and these rules, and submit. Receiving marketing e-mail is a separate, optional choice that is off by default and never affects eligibility.
  3. Tap the confirmation link in the e-mail you receive. It works once and expires 24 hours after it is sent.
  4. Your entry is confirmed and you receive an entry reference number (format L66-XXXX-XXXX).

4. Round structure and limits

Each round accepts at most 200 verified entries. A round opens and closes at the dates announced for that round on the entry page. If fewer than 200 entries are received, the round still closes at the announced deadline and the prize is awarded according to these rules. Additional rounds may be created only by the sponsor's authorized administrator.

5. Prize

One (1) $66 KPOT EXPRESS store-credit code per round (approximate retail value $66.00 USD). No physical card is issued: the code carries a $66 balance that is deducted automatically on each order until it is used up. If the remaining balance is less than an order total, the difference may be paid with another payment method. After every successful payment the remaining balance is shown to you. Code restrictions, expiry, cash-back and redemption conditions are governed by these Official Rules and by Georgia law; the code is redeemable only at KPOT EXPRESS and is not exchangeable or transferable for cash. The code expires 12 months after issuance if unused.

6. Drawing method and odds

When a round closes, one entry is selected by a cryptographically secure random draw performed on the sponsor's server from a frozen snapshot of all verified entries. Every verified entry has exactly the same probability of selection; odds depend on the number of verified entries in the round (e.g., 1 in 200 at capacity). No human selection, weighting or preference is part of the process. The draw and its result are recorded in an administrative audit log.

7. Winner notification and claim

  • The winner is notified by e-mail at the address that entered, with the entry reference and claim instructions.
  • The winner must claim within 14 days of notification (reasonable and confirmed by counsel) by replying to the notification or calling the shop at (678) 335-5388.
  • Alternate winner: if a selected winner cannot be reached, fails to claim within the window, or is found ineligible, an alternate winner is drawn at random from the remaining verified entries under the same method.
  • Winner information may be required for prize fulfillment and tax purposes where applicable. Prizes are not transferable except by the sponsor.

8. Privacy

We collect your e-mail address, the time of entry, a one-way reference to your address for duplicate checking, and your optional marketing choice. We do not publish entrant e-mail addresses. Marketing e-mail is sent only to people who opt in, always includes sender information and an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribe requests are honored promptly. See the LUCKY 66 Privacy Policy for details.

Any attempt to enter with multiple addresses or by automated means may be disqualified. These rules are governed by Georgia law. Sponsor's decisions on administration are final to the extent permitted by law. Any disputes arising under these rules shall be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Cobb County, Georgia, and the parties consent to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.
